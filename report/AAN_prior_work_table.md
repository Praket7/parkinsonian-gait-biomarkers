# Where this project fits

The studies below answer related but different questions. Sample sizes and outcomes are reported as each source defines them. This table is a comparison of methods, not a ranking of studies.

| Study | N | Measurement | Outcome | External validation | Reliability | Beyond-speed test | Main gap relative to this project |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tan et al., 2019 [4] | 62 people with PD | Kinect-derived gait and modified Timed Up and Go | UPDRS motor and PIGD associations | Not reported as an external cohort test | Not the principal test | Yes. Only vertical pelvic displacement added to habitual gait speed for PIGD association | Association and incremental fit were studied, not a frozen control-reference score across a full analytical and longitudinal evidence ladder |
| Jakob et al., 2021 [5] | 37 PD; 14 controls | Wearable gait system compared with 3D motion capture | Measurement agreement and PD-control differences | No independent clinical-site transport | Agreement and reliability statistics | No | Technical agreement was feature-specific and did not establish long-term score stability |
| Bouça-Machado et al., 2020 [7] | 95 studies in review | Technology-based gait measures | Spatiotemporal gait characteristics | Studies were heterogeneous | Reliability was not harmonized across all included studies | Not a single pooled beyond-speed test | The review identified variation in protocols and the need for work on clinical meaning and free-living progression |
| Micó-Amigo et al., 2023 [8] | Consortium validation program | Real-world digital mobility outcomes | Fit-for-purpose measurement validation | Multi-condition, multi-site program | A staged validation framework | Depends on the outcome and DMO | Establishes a validation roadmap, not validity of the present WearGait score |
| Yarnall et al., 2025 [9] | 531 PD and 221 controls with usable DMO data | Seven-day wearable real-world mobility | Group and disease-stage differences across 24 DMOs | Large consortium cohort | Feasibility and compliance emphasized | Not the same speed-only incremental test | Supports real-world DMO relevance but does not validate this eight-input score |
| Adeli et al., 2025 [10] | 362 people across 9 cohorts and 8 centers in CARE-PD | Anonymized SMPL body-mesh gait sequences from video and motion capture | UPDRS gait estimation and motion benchmarks | Within-dataset, cross-dataset, leave-one-dataset-out, and adaptation protocols | Not equivalent to the present score’s repeated-session ICC test | Uses model baselines, not this preregistered speed comparison | CARE-PD has valuable labels and site diversity, but the frozen PKMAS inputs need a separate validated measurement bridge |
| This project, v4.5 evidence | 62 PD in primary severity analysis; 252 matched bridge trials; 89 four-pass longitudinal pairs by task | Control-referenced eight-input walkway score | Concurrent gait-specific MDS-UPDRS association, rank information, analytical agreement, and long-interval stability | One-site sensitivity; full CARE-PD frozen-score bridge not estimable | Six-plus-month score stability tested; short-term reliability not estimable | Participant-grouped repeated validation | Brings association, reference robustness, ranking, analytical validity, and long-interval stability into one prespecified evidence ladder |
